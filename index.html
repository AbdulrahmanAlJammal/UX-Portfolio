CASE 01 SKYFLARES OPENED 2024 STATUS: SHIPPED
[ SkyFlares interface shot goes here ]

SkyFlares

AI storm-prediction platform · UX Research + Product Design
EVIDENCE

Mixed-method research showed one forecast serving three opposed audiences: researchers wanted raw depth, policymakers wanted decisions, the public wanted plain language.

ANALYSIS
DECIDED
One tiered alert system, three reading depths
REJECTED
Three separate dashboards per audience
BECAUSE
Shared truth beats fragmented views; maintenance triples with three UIs
Verdict: Usable science

Storm timers + simplified alert levels kept scientific accuracy without demanding a physics degree.

How I think about the work

01

Outcomes over deliverables

Wireframes are not the job. The job is the number that moves after they ship. Every case file here is framed by what changed, not what was drawn.

02

Trade-offs, shown honestly

Good design is choosing what to lose. My decision logs show the option I rejected and why. Judgment is the skill; process is just its trail.

03

Learn the system, then design it

I took a role inside HSBC's compliance operation to understand banking from the operator's seat. You design better screens when you know what happens behind them.
