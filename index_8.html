02 / CASE ARCHIVE

Four cases. You are inside one.

CASE C-01 / SKYFLARES OPENED 2024 ROLE: UX RESEARCH + PRODUCT DESIGN STATUS: SHIPPED

EVIDENCE

Mixed-method research showed one forecast serving three opposed audiences: researchers wanted raw depth, policymakers wanted decisions, the public wanted plain language.

ANALYSIS

Decided: one tiered alert system with three reading depths, from a live storm countdown to full model data. Rejected: three separate dashboards, because shared truth beats fragmented views and triple maintenance.

Verdict: usable science

Storm timers and simplified alert levels kept NASA-grade accuracy without demanding a physics degree.

CASE C-02 / THE UNITED BANK SELF-INITIATED REDESIGN ROLE: UI/UX DESIGNER STATUS: CONCEPT

EVIDENCE

A national bank's web presence served retail, corporate and Islamic banking audiences while burying the everyday errands: cards, loans, finding a branch. Visitors arrive with tasks, not curiosity.

ANALYSIS

Decided: a task-first homepage where loans, cards and branches sit one tap deep, responsive from desktop to phone, bilingual-ready. Rejected: the product-brochure structure, because nobody browses a bank for fun. Self-initiated trial: no client brief, my own constraints.

Exhibit: fintech

The redesign that pointed my compass at fintech, two years before I joined a bank to learn the systems behind the screens.

CASE C-03 / QUDURAT 2024 · 700 APPS ROLE: UI/UX DESIGNER STATUS: SHIPPED

EVIDENCE

Government universities needed one Arabic-first platform for HR capability services: faculty users, formal context, full right-to-left reading logic.

ANALYSIS

Decided: designed RTL from the first wireframe within the Saudi DGA design system, and rebuilt onboarding after usability testing exposed early drop-off. Rejected: mirroring an LTR layout, because RTL is a reading logic, not a flipped image.

+40% engagement

New-user engagement rose 40% after the rebuilt first-run flow shipped, validated by post-launch analytics.

EXHIBIT EX-01 / CINEMAPHILE ROLE: UX RESEARCH + UI DESIGN STATUS: PROTOTYPE

BRIEF

Buying snacks mid-movie means missing the movie. CinemaPhile lets viewers scan the QR code on their seat, order from their phone, and have snacks delivered to that seat, or schedule them to be ready before the film starts.

THE CALL

The seat scan removes the one question delivery needs answered: where are you? And the interface stays dark on purpose, so a lit phone disappears inside a dark theater.

Seat-side delivery
EXHIBIT EX-02 / GUSTO ROLE: UI/UX DESIGN SCOPE: DASHBOARD + 6 CRUD MODULES

BRIEF

The listener app gets the spotlight; the people running it usually get spreadsheets. Gusto's admin panel gives operators a live dashboard and full management tables for artists, albums, users, ads and subscription plans.

THE CALL

Charts answer one question, how is the platform doing; tables answer another, what needs fixing. The layout never mixes the two jobs, and every destructive action sits one deliberate click away from its row.

Built for operators

03 / METHOD

How cases get closed

My method is Human-Centered Design: every decision anchored in human evidence, structured in layers, and run as an iterative problem-solving cycle rather than a straight line.

Outcomes over deliverables

The job is the number that moves after shipping, not the wireframes before it.

Trade-offs on the record

Every case shows the rejected option and why. Judgment is the skill.

Learn the system first

I went inside a bank to design better for banking. Screens serve systems.

THE LAYERS · FROM STRATEGY TO SURFACE

Every interface is the top of a stack. I work the layers in order, so surface choices always trace back to strategy.

05 · SURFACEVisual design: type, color, motion. What people call "the design."
04 · SKELETONInterface and navigation: layout, wayfinding, information placement.
03 · STRUCTUREInteraction design and information architecture: how it behaves and organizes.
02 · SCOPEFeatures and content: what gets built, and what gets rejected.
01 · STRATEGYUser needs and business goals: why this exists at all.

THE LOOP · A PROBLEM-SOLVING CYCLE

The layers give the structure; the loop gives the motion. Six moves, repeated until the evidence says done.

01DISCOVERResearch first: interviews, analytics, context. Evidence before opinions.
02DEFINEFrame the real problem: personas, jobs to be done, success metrics set upfront.
03IDEATEDiverge on purpose: multiple concepts before converging, trade-offs on the record.
04PROTOTYPEWireframes to hi-fi in a token system, built to be tested, not admired.
05TESTUsability sessions with real users; the prototype faces evidence.
06LEARN & SHIPMeasure the outcome, keep what works, file what did not.
↻ THEN BACK TO 01 · THE CYCLE CLOSES CASES, A LINE NEVER DOES
In practice the cycle bends to each project's constraints: SkyFlares ran two full loops before the alert system held; Qudurat's loop was compressed by the DGA system but never skipped testing, and that is where the +40% came from.
